United States

Tourism, family, business, study and renewals.

Book your consultation Tell us about your case

What you should know

It is one of the most requested destinations from Guatemala. The process usually includes an online form, payment of an official fee and, in many cases, a consular interview.

Each application is assessed individually. That is why, rather than memorising answers, what matters is that your application is truthful, consistent and reflects your current situation.

What applies to your country

Nationals of Guatemala need a visa to travel to the United States, because this country does not take part in the Visa Waiver Program.

U.S. Department of State: visas. Source reviewed on November 8, 2026. These rules change. Before you travel or pay a fee, confirm them with the official source.

On the day of your appointment

What the embassy’s consular section in Guatemala asks for at the nonimmigrant visa interview. You may be asked for additional documents depending on your case.

What to bring

  1. Your valid passport, valid for at least six months beyond the time you will stay in the United States.
  2. The passport containing your most recent US visa, if you have had one.
  3. The confirmation page of your DS-160 form.
  4. Your printed appointment confirmation, with its instructions.
  5. A 5 × 5 cm colour photograph taken in the last six months.
  6. The original documents that support the purpose of your trip.

Before you leave home

  • You can only enter with a scheduled appointment, and everyone goes through a security check.
  • The embassy restricts electronic devices. Check the current list and decide beforehand who will keep your phone.
  • The embassy is at Boulevard Austriaco 11-51, zona 16, Guatemala City. Allow for traffic and arrive early.
  • Do not buy tickets or finalise travel plans until your visa has been issued. The consular section itself recommends this.

Words you will hear

These terms appear in forms, emails and consulate replies. It helps to understand them before you start.

DS-160
The online form you use to apply for a nonimmigrant visa. What you write there must match what you say at the interview.
Consular interview
The conversation with the consular officer who decides your application. They ask about your trip and your situation, and your answers must match your form.
Renewal without interview
Some people can renew their visa by submitting their documents without attending an interview. It depends on your previous visa and the current rules.
214(b)
The most common refusal. It means the officer was not convinced that you meet the requirements of the visa you applied for. There is no appeal, but you can apply again if your situation has changed or you have new information.
221(g)
A temporary refusal: information is missing or your case needs administrative processing. If you are asked for documents, you have one year to submit them before you have to apply and pay again.

Questions about this destination

How much does the US visa cost?

The official fee changes and is published by the Department of State. We show you the current figure from the official source. Our fees are separate and we explain them before we start.

How long are appointment waits in Guatemala?

Waiting times change constantly. Check the official wait times on the Department of State website. We help you find them.

Can I renew my US visa without an interview?

Some people may qualify, depending on their previous visa and the current rules. We check it with you against the official source.
