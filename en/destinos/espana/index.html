Spain

Study, work and long stays; short visits depending on your nationality.

Book your consultation Tell us about your case

What you should know

Spain is part of the Schengen Area: for visits of up to 90 days in any 180-day period the same rule applies as in the rest of the area, and many Central American nationalities do not need a visa for those stays.

To study, work, join your family or stay longer, you apply for a Spanish national visa at the consulate that serves your place of residence. It is a document-based process with timelines worth planning well in advance.

What applies to your country

Nationals of Guatemala are among those exempt from a visa for stays of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. To study, work or stay longer, a national visa from the relevant country is usually required.

European Union: Regulation 2018/1806, Annex II. Source reviewed on November 8, 2026. These rules change. Before you travel or pay a fee, confirm them with the official source.

Related visa types

Short stay (Schengen)
For visits of up to 90 days. Many Central American nationalities are visa-exempt.
Study visa
For longer courses and programmes at Spanish institutions.
Work visa
Usually linked to an employer or a work authorisation.
Residence and family reunification
To live in Spain because of family ties or other reasons; each type has its own requirements.

Questions about this destination

I am from Guatemala. Do I need a visa for a holiday in Spain?

For stays of up to 90 days in any 180-day period, Guatemalan nationality is among those exempt from a Schengen visa. Even so, at the border you may be asked to justify your trip.

Where do I apply for a study or work visa?

At the Spanish consulate that serves your place of residence. We help you identify the visa and organise the documents you are asked for.
