Where do you want to travel?

Every country has its own rules, and they also depend on your nationality. Start with the destination you have in mind.

What each country asks of you depends on your nationality, where you live, the type of visa and your situation. That is why for each destination we show what applies to your country and link the official source.

  • United States Tourism, family, business, study and renewals. We advise on: Tourism and business (B1/B2) · Student (F, M) · Temporary work · Renewals See destination
  • Canada Visits, study and guidance for work trips. We advise on: Visitor visa · Study permit · Work permit See destination
  • Mexico Visits and business trips to our neighbouring country. We advise on: Visitor without paid activities · Temporary residence See destination
  • Schengen Area Europe: short stays, study and longer stays. We advise on: Short stay (C visa) · National study visa · National work or residence visa See destination
  • Spain Study, work and long stays; short visits depending on your nationality. We advise on: Short stay (Schengen) · Study visa · Work visa · Residence and family reunification See destination
  • Germany Study, vocational training and skilled work. We advise on: Short stay (Schengen) · Study visa · Vocational training · Skilled work See destination
  • Switzerland Visits, study and residence permits. We advise on: Short stay (Schengen) · Study · Work See destination
  • United Kingdom Visits with an ETA or a visa depending on your country, study and work. We advise on: ETA · Visitor visa · Student visa · Work visa See destination
  • Australia Visits, study and long trips to the other side of the world. We advise on: Visitor (subclass 600) · Student (subclass 500) · Work See destination
