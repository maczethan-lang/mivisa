How we help

These are the four travel purposes people ask us about most. For each one we explain what your application requires and prepare it with you.

  • Tourist visa To get to know another country or visit your family. We guide you as you prepare your application and help you identify the documents you need.
  • Business visa For meetings, trade fairs, training and short work trips. We help you explain clearly what you will do on your trip and what supports it.
  • Student visa If you have been accepted into a programme abroad, or are about to choose one. We organise the deadlines, your documents and how you explain your study plan with you.
  • Work visa If you have a job offer or an employer in another country. We help you understand which process applies and spot suspicious offers.

Two people can apply for the same visa and need very different things. That is why we first listen to your situation and then tell you what makes sense.

Tell us about your case
How much does an advisory session cost?

It depends on your case and the help you need. We tell you in writing before we start, so you can decide calmly. Official application fees are separate.

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No, and nobody should promise you that. The decision belongs only to the immigration authority. What we do is help you present your application clearly and well prepared.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the country, the type of visa and the season, and the timelines are set by each authority. We review the current official information for your case with you.

Can you help me if my visa was refused?

Yes. We review what happened and what has changed since then, and tell you honestly whether it makes sense to apply again now or wait.

Do I need all my documents before contacting you?

No. You can write to us even if you are only thinking about travelling. We help you work out what you need.

What do I need for the first conversation?

Just tell us about your travel plan and whether you have a valid passport. Everything else depends on your case and we will let you know afterwards.

What is the difference between an advisory and the application at the embassy?

The advisory is our job: guiding you and helping you prepare. The official application is made to the embassy or authority of the country, which reviews it and decides. We are not part of either.

Can I receive advisory from another country?

Yes. The whole advisory can be remote, by WhatsApp and video call, from anywhere in Guatemala or from another country. The only in-person part is your appointment at the embassy or consulate, when one is required.
